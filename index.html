    05 · GEAR

    Gear and loot changes

    > the short version

    Tier 1 is all new. Every class gets a 6-piece set for each role, at item level 65, from Hyjal Summit and Barrow Deeps. Four of the six slots can also be crafted at item level 61, but only with essence from disenchanted raid loot. Every stat beyond armour stays hidden until the piece first drops on our realm.

    How Tier 1 works

    • 6 pieces, 4 bonuses at 2, 3, 4 and 5 pieces. The 4-piece adds damage against one creature type.
    • Head and chest only drop in raids. The other four slots also drop in raids.
    • Binds to the looter. Which boss drops which slot isn't published yet.

    Crafted pieces

    • Shoulders, hands, legs, feet at item level 61, made by Blacksmithing, Leatherworking or Tailoring at 300.
    • Needs Malleable Essence of Nature, which only comes from disenchanting new raid loot, plus Guardians of Hyjal reputation and a camp station.
    • Wear 1 crafted piece, or 2 after buying the Rousing Seed Pod (10 gold).
