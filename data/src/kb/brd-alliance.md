# Ultimate BRD Quest Guide (Alliance) — WoW Forever
-# Most BRD quests need level 48–50 to accept (The Challenge needs 58). Quests 48+ also pay gold in Forever.

## Phase 1: Preparation
**1. The Love Potion (anyone) — needs a second trip**{q}
- **Requires:** 4x Gromsblood, 10x Giant Silver Vein (cliff giants in Azshara), 1x Nagmara's Filled Vial
- Mistress Nagmara (Grim Guzzler, inside BRD) gives you an empty **Nagmara's Vial**{i} when you accept. Fill it at **Golakka Hot Springs** in Un'Goro Crater.
- You can only finish it this trip if you picked it up on an earlier visit. Otherwise grab it in Run 2 and bring everything next time.

**2. The Spectral Chalice (Miners, skill 230+)**{q}
- **Requires:** 2x Star Ruby, 20x Gold Bar, 10x Truesilver Bar
- Talk to **Gloom'rel**{n} in the Summoners' Tomb **before** anyone talks to Doom'rel and starts the Seven. Put the mats in the floating **Spectral Chalice**, then talk to Gloom'rel to learn **Smelt Dark Iron**.

**3. Onyxia attunement, up to "Marshal Windsor"**{q}
- **Helendis Riverhorn**{n} (Morgan's Vigil): **Dragonkin Menace**{q}, then the six-step **The True Masters**{q} chain (Lakeshire and Stormwind). It ends at **Marshal Maxwell**{n}, who gives **Marshal Windsor**{q}.

**4. The Challenge (level 58+, Valthalak / Dungeon Set 2 chain only)**{q}
- From **Falrin Treeshaper**{n} (Dire Maul West library), after **Anthion's Old Friend**{q} → **The Instigator's Enchantment**{q}.
- In BRD: plant the **Banner of Provocation**{i} in the Ring of Law while Grimstone sentences you, kill Theldren's team, and take the **Top Piece of Lord Valthalak's Amulet**{i} to Anthion Harmon (Eastern Plaguelands).
- Not on that chain? Skip it.

## Phase 2: Quest Pickup Tour (part 1)
-# Starts and ends in Ironforge. There's one backtrack, and it saves you a third BRD run.

**1. Ironforge — The High Seat**
- **Royal Historian Archesonus**{n} (38.5, 55.5), next to Magni: **The Smoldering Ruins of Thaurissan**{q}. Part 1 is listening to his story. Part 2 sends you to the Ruins of Thaurissan in Burning Steppes.

**2. Dun Morogh — Kharanos**
- **Ragnar Thunderbrew**{n} (46.8, 52.5), Thunderbrew Distillery: **Hurley Blackbreath**{q}
- *Optional:* Enohar Thunderbrew (Nethergarde Keep, Blasted Lands) has the breadcrumb **Ragnar Thunderbrew**{q}. Not needed.

**3. Tanaris — Steamwheedle Port (optional)**
- **Yorba Screwspigot**{n} (67, 24): breadcrumb **Yuka Screwspigot**{q}. Not needed, Yuka gives the Ribbly quest anyway.

**4. Burning Steppes — Morgan's Vigil**
- **Marshal Maxwell**{n} (84.6, 68.8): **Marshal Windsor**{q}
- **Oralius**{n} (84.5, 68.5): **The Good Stuff**{q} (20 Dark Iron Fanny Packs, from any Dark Iron dwarf in BRD)
- **Jalinda Sprig**{n} (85.4, 70.0): **Overmaster Pyron**{q} (kill him on your way into Run 1, not now)

## Phase 2: Quest Pickup Tour (part 2)

**5. Cyrus Therepentous**{n} — Slither Rock cave, Burning Steppes (94.8, 31.6)
He wants a **Black Dragonflight Molt**{i} before he gives **A Taste of Flame**{q} (kill Bael'Gar).
- **Fast way:** talk to Cyrus without a molt and a **Frenzied Black Drake**{h} (level 54 elite) shows up. Kill it, loot the molt, show it to him.
- **Long way:** the **Divine Retribution**{q} chain in Searing Gorge, from **Velarok Windblade**{n} (39.0, 38.7). The molt is in the hoard at the end.
- Either way you leave with the **Altered Black Dragonflight Molt**{i} and **A Taste of Flame**{q}.

**6. Flame Crest** (neutral goblins)
- **Maxwort Uberglint**{n} (65.2, 23.8): **The Heart of the Mountain**{q}
- **Yuka Screwspigot**{n} (66, 22): **Ribbly Screwspigot**{q} (turn in the Tanaris breadcrumb first if you have it)

**7. Ruins of Thaurissan**
- Click the **Thaurissan Relics** for part 2 of *The Smoldering Ruins of Thaurissan*{q}.

**8. Back to Ironforge**
- Turn in *The Smoldering Ruins of Thaurissan*{q} to Archesonus. **King Magni**{n} then gives **Kharan Mighthammer**{q}.
- Without it you can't do Kharan in Run 1, and the princess chain costs a third run.

## Phase 3: Blackrock Mountain Essentials

**1. Dark Iron Legacy (the ghost quest)**{q}
- In Blackrock Mountain, **die** (jump in the lava).
- As a ghost, run to the coffin hanging on chains over the Molten Span and talk to **Franclorn Forgewright**{n}.
- Listening to his story finishes part 1, and he gives you part 2.

**2. Attunement to the Core**{q}
- Resurrect and find **Lothos Riftwaker**{n} on the path down to the BRD portal. Pick up **Attunement to the Core**{q}.
- Molten Core isn't open yet, but you can grab the Core Fragment now so you're attuned when it is.

**3. Overmaster Pyron**{q}
- Kill **Pyron**{h} (level 52 elite fire elemental) in the Blackrock Quarry, right outside the BRD portal. Credit is shared, so do it as a group.

## Phase 4: Dungeon Run 1 (The Setup)
*Goal: trigger the two "leave the dungeon" steps (Windsor and the Princess).*

**1. Enter BRD → Detention Block.**
**2. High Interrogator Gerstahn:**{h} kill her and loot the **Prison Cell Key**{i}. It opens the cells, and one keyholder can open a door for the whole group.
**3. Marshal Windsor**{n} (his cell): turn in *Marshal Windsor*{q}, accept **Abandoned Hope**{q}.
**4. Kharan Mighthammer**{n} (open his cell with the key): turn in *Kharan Mighthammer*{q}, do **Kharan's Tale**{q} (go through all his dialogue), accept **The Bearer of Bad News**{q}.
**5. Keep** any **Relic Coffer Keys**{i} and **Dark Iron Fanny Packs**{i} that drop. They count in Run 2.
**6. Leave the instance.** No need for the Ring of Law yet.

## Phase 6: Dungeon Run 2 (part 1): front half + Jail Break!
-# Angerforge and Argelmach are close to the entrance through the Shadowforge Lock. You get Windsor's pages early and escort him before heading deep, so there's no run back from the throne room.

**1. Detention Block:** farm Dark Iron dwarves until **A Crumpled Up Note**{i} drops. It can't be shared in Forever, so everyone needs their own. Turn it in to Windsor and accept **A Shred of Hope**{q}.
**2. Ring of Law:** clear it. If you have *The Challenge*{q}, plant the banner while Grimstone sentences you.
**3. The Black Vault:** open all 12 **Relic Coffers** with Relic Coffer Keys (dwarves drop them). Kill **Watchman Doomgrip**{h} and the **Warbringer Constructs**{h}, then open the **Secret Safe** under the Dark Coffer for **The Heart of the Mountain**{i}. Everyone can loot it since the 9 Nov hotfix.
**4. Fineous Darkvire**{h} (Hall of Crafting): loot **Ironfel**{i} and put it on **Franclorn's monument** in the Shrine of Thaurissan. That finishes **Dark Iron Legacy**{q} and gives you the **Shadowforge Key**{i}.
**5. Bael'Gar**{h} (Dark Iron Highway): kill him, then use the **Altered Black Dragonflight Molt**{i} on his remains for the **Encased Fiery Essence**{i}.
**6. Lord Incendius**{h} (Black Anvil): kill him for **Incendius!**{q}
**7. General Angerforge:**{h} open the **Shadowforge Lock** with the key (room off the Ring of Law's west gate), go upstairs to him. Loot **Marshal Windsor's Lost Information**{i}.
**8. Golem Lord Argelmach**{h} (next room over): kill his two constructs first, then him. Loot the second **Lost Information**{i}.
**9. Jail Break!:**{q} short walk back to Windsor. Everyone turns in *A Shred of Hope*{q}, then accept **Jail Break!**{q} to start the escort. He walks through the Detention Block and the Ring of Law to get his gear and free his friends. He **can't be healed**, so taunt the ambushers off him.

## Phase 6: Dungeon Run 2 (part 2): the deep end

**10. The Grim Guzzler (bar):** keep the bar friendly until you're done (don't attack Plugger or the patrons).
- **Hurley Blackbreath:**{h} break the three **Thunderbrew Lager Kegs** in the storeroom, kill Hurley and his cronies, loot the **Lost Thunderbrew Recipe**{i}.
- **Ribbly Screwspigot:**{h} kill him (his cousins join in) and loot **Ribbly's Head**{i}.
- **Mistress Nagmara:**{n} turn in *The Love Potion*{q} if you brought everything, or pick it up for next time.

**11. The Seven**{h} (Summoners' Tomb): miners do *The Spectral Chalice*{q} with Gloom'rel **first**. Killing the Seven opens the way on.

**12. Core Fragment** (*Attunement to the Core*{q}): past the Seven, the big gate straight ahead leads to the Lyceum. Take the path on the **right** instead (two packs of fire elementals) to the green Molten Core portal. The fragment is the stalagmite to the left of the portal.

**13. The Lyceum → Magmus.**

**14. Emperor Dagran Thaurissan**{h}
⚠️ **Do NOT kill Princess Moira.** She heals him, so interrupt and CC her, but keep her alive.
- Once he's dead she turns friendly. Turn in *The Fate of the Kingdom*{q} and accept **The Princess's Surprise**{q}.

**15. Hearth out.** Nothing left to go back for.
